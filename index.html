Our Services

We provide respectful, confidential and evidence-based care. Below are the main services offered at Frontlane Medical Center.

1. Abortion & Post-abortion care

  • Legal / Safe abortion
  • Post-abortive care
  • Missed abortion
  • Inevitable abortion
  • Induced failed abortion
  • Surgical abortion
  • Manual Vacuum Aspiration / Evacuation (MVA/MVE)
  • Miscarriage management
  • Non-viable pregnancy management

2. Antenatal & Maternity

Antenatal checkups, birth preparedness, labor support and postnatal follow-up.

3. Family Planning

All modern contraceptive methods provided with counseling and follow-up.

4. Ultrasound Scan

Obstetric and pelvic ultrasound scans with experienced sonographers.

5. Minor Surgeries

Outpatient minor procedures under local anesthesia.

6. Admissions & General Medicine

Inpatient care for medical conditions, observation and acute management.
